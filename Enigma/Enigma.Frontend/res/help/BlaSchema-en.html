Invisible Luminaries Astrology Schema

Invisible Luminaries Astrology (ILA) is a specialized form of astrology developed by the Dutch astrologer George Bode. ILA uses multiple celestial points in the chart that are unknown in other forms of astrology. It is important to know that the rulership of the signs differs significantly from what is customary. In the appendix of the user manual, you will find a brief summary of ILA.

This schema contains counts of various factors in the chart.

Settings

For the calculation of this schema, you can choose different settings.

For the corrected Black Moon, you will see Corrected Duval by default, but you can also choose Corrected SE or Interpolated.

For the house system, you see the default selection Placidus. You can change this to another system. The choice is limited to quadrant systems (with the Ascendant equal to cusp 1 and the MC equal to cusp 10).

The four checkboxes offer you the following choices:

  • Use the True Node instead of the Mean Node.
  • Include Chiron in the calculations.
  • Include Ceres in the calculations.
  • Use decanates in the counts for dispositors.

If you change any of the options, Enigma will automatically recalculate the schema.

Positions

In the left column, you see the positions of the planets and the cusps. Next to the longitude, you see the house and the decanate. Enigma does not take into account the choices you made in the configuration. The celestial points included are predetermined; you can only indicate whether you want to include Chiron and Ceres.

Elements

Under Elements, you will find the counts of celestial points in fire, earth, air, and water signs. Additionally, the counts in the analogous houses, and then the sum of these two values. Under Hcusp are the totals of celestial points in houses whose cusp is in a sign of the indicated element.

Crosses

Under Crosses (modalities), Enigma shows the crosses: cardinal, fixed, and mutable. The setup is the same as for Elements.

Dispositors

The Dispositors table shows, on the left, two celestial points that each rule over the same two signs.

Under the Comb column, you see the counts of planets in signs that are ruled by the two indicated points.

The Signs column shows the totals in signs and is essentially the sum of both numbers in the Comb column.

The Ind column shows the totals of the indirect rulerships: the totals of celestial points in signs that are ruled by points which are in a sign ruled by one of the rulers in the left column. These two rulers themselves are not counted.

Directly after the "Ind" column, you see the "Sum" column containing the addition of the "Signs" and "Ind" columns.

For houses - the H'ses column - an analogous story applies. In the second to last column, Dec., you find the counts for the decans, provided you have selected these in the options. For decans, only direct rulership applies; there is no Ind column. Finally, you see the Total of the two Sum columns and the decans.

Underneath the table with dispositors, Enigma shows a histogram with the totals of all dispositors.

Quadrants

Under Quadrants, you will find the number of points located in one of the quadrants: quadrant 1 (houses 1,2,3), quadrant 2 (houses 4,5,6), quadrant 3 (houses 7,8,9), and quadrant 4 (houses 10,11,12).

Details

The Details table contains multiple, self-contained, specific features.

  • Sister Sign Asc. shows the number and symbol of the sign that is ruled by the same sign pair as the sign on the ascendant.
  • Clamped Houses contains the clamped houses; houses that begin and end in the same sign.
  • Intercepted Signs shows the numbers and symbols of intercepted signs.
  • Ground note displays successively the following houses: the ascendant, the house that is analogous to the sign on the ascendant, the house with the sister sign on the cusp, and any second house with the same sign as the ascendant on the cusp.
  • Lords Asc. in houses shows the houses where the two rulers of the ascendant are located.
  • Moon in house shows, you guessed it, the house where the Moon is located.

Cycles

For each cross and each element, you will find here the rulers that, within that cross or element, are in each other's signs.

Shortened Cycles

As Cycles, but now for rulers of the same ruler pair.

In own sign

Points that are in their own sign.

In own house

Points that are in the house they rule.

In own mundane house

Points that are in the house they rule mundanely (the house that is analogous to the sign the point rules).

Receptions

There are three types of receptions: in signs, in houses, and in mundane houses.

  • A reception in sign applies when two points are each in a sign that is ruled by the other point.
  • For houses, it applies when two points are each in a house that is ruled by the other point.
  • Receptions in mundane houses are seen when two planets are each in a house that is ruled mundanely by the other point.
