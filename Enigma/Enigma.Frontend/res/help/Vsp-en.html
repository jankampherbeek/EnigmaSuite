Venus Star Point

This screen shows the Venus Star Point (VSP) calculations for the selected chart.

Chart Wheel

The left side displays a graphic wheel showing the VSP positions. The wheel shows the Sun at the longitude of each VSP (since Sun and Venus are at the same longitude during conjunction).

You can use the checkboxes at the bottom to modify the display:

  • No time: Removes houses and angles from the chart
  • No sign colors: Uses a neutral background for the signs

Use the button Export to create an export of the chart in png-format.

Venus Star Point Positions

The right side shows a detailed table of VSP positions, including:

  • Name: names for the positions. The last position before birth is the Head. The positions before and after the Head are called: Arm. The first and last positions are the Legs.
  • Sequence: The chronological sequence number of the point.
  • Date/time (UT): The date and time (in UT) of the VSP.
  • Conjunction: Either an Inferior or Superior conjunction.
  • Longitude: The ecliptical longitude of the Venus Star Point

About Venus Star Points

The Venus Star Point has been introduced by Arielle Guttman and shows the subsequent conjunctions between the Sun and Venus. These conjunctions result in a pentagram figure.

There are two types of conjunctions:

  • Inferior Conjunction: Venus passes between Earth and the Sun
  • Superior Conjunction: Venus passes behind the Sun from Earth's perspective

These points are significant in astrology and can be used for timing and interpretation purposes.
