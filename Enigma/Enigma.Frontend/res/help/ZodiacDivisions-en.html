Zodiac divisions

Zodiac Divisions displays the longitude, sign, decan, dodecatemoria, and bounds of the selected celestial bodies.

You can choose from multiple variants. When you change a selection, the table content updates automatically.

Decans

Each zodiac sign consists of 3 decans of 10° each. These 10-degree segments can be linked to planets or to signs.

Dodecatemoria

Originally, dodecatemoria consist of a twelvefold division of the zodiac signs. Each sign starts with itself, followed by the next sign, and so on. This method is called Original.

A less common variant is found in the work of Paulus Alexandrinus. Here, you multiply the position within the sign by 12 and add it to the original position—effectively multiplying by 13 (the 13th harmonic). This method is called Paulus.

For dodecatemoria, you may also encounter the term 'Dwads'.

Bounds

Bounds (or terms) are divisions of the signs into 5 unequal parts, each ruled by a planet. There are many variants of this system. The Egyptian version is the most commonly used, but there is also a variant introduced by Ptolemy. You can choose between Egyptian and Ptolemy for their respective versions.

The systems mentioned here originate from the time when astrology was sidereal. If you wish to calculate sidereal positions, select your preferred Ayanamsha in the configuration. In the configuration, you can also determine which celestial bodies and points to include in the overview.

Your current selections are briefly displayed above the table.
