About Enigma

Enigma is a program that facilitates the use of astrology and astrological research. It is based on the Swiss Ephemeris (SE), a library of astrological calculations.

Use the help buttons, or the user manual, to learn more about the possibilities of Enigma.

Many people contributed to the development of Enigma, by technical advice, suggestions, reporting errors and otherwise.
