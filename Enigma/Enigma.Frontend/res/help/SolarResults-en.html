Solar Return Results

This screen shows the results of a solar return calculation.

Solar Chart Tab

The Solar Chart tab displays a graphic wheel of the solar return chart. This shows the positions of all planets and points at the time of the solar return.

You can use the checkboxes at the bottom to modify the display:

  • No time: Removes houses and angles from the chart
  • No aspects: Removes aspect lines from the chart
  • No sign colors: Uses a neutral background for the signs

Use the button Export to create an export of the chart in png-format.

Positions Tab

The Positions tab shows a detailed table of all planetary positions in the solar return chart, including:

  • Longitude: The ecliptical longitude of each point
  • Latitude: The ecliptical latitude of each point
  • Right ascension: The equatorial right ascension
  • Declination: The equatorial declination

Aspects with Radix Tab

The Aspects with Radix tab shows the aspects between the solar return positions and the natal (radix) positions. This helps identify which solar return planets are forming aspects to the natal chart.

The table shows:

  • Solar: The planet in the solar return chart
  • Aspect: The type of aspect formed
  • Radix: The planet in the natal chart
  • Effective orb: The orb of the aspect

You can change the orb and calculate the aspects.

Details

The Details tab shows the calculated times and other technical information for this solar.
