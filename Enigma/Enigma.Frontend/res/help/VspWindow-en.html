Venus Star Point

This screen shows the Venus Star Point calculations for the selected chart.

Chart Wheel

The left side displays a graphic wheel showing the Venus Star Point positions. The wheel shows the Sun at the longitude of each Venus Star Point (since Sun and Venus are at the same longitude during conjunction).

You can use the checkboxes at the bottom to modify the display:

  • No time: Removes houses and angles from the chart
  • No aspects: Removes aspect lines from the chart
  • No sign colors: Uses a neutral background for the signs

Use the button Export to create an export of the chart in png-format.

Venus Star Point Positions

The right side shows a detailed table of Venus Star Point positions, including:

  • Sequence: The chronological sequence number of the point
  • Date: The date of the Venus Star Point
  • Time: The time of the Venus Star Point
  • Phenomenon: Whether it's an Inferior or Superior conjunction
  • Longitude: The ecliptical longitude of the Venus Star Point

PreNatal Checkbox

The PreNatal checkbox controls whether to include prenatal Venus Star Points in the calculation:

  • Unchecked: Shows only postnatal Venus Star Points (starting from the first one after birth)
  • Checked: Includes one prenatal Venus Star Point and shows postnatal points

When you change this setting, the calculation is automatically performed again with the new parameter.

About Venus Star Points

Venus Star Points occur when Venus forms a conjunction with the Sun. There are two types:

  • Inferior Conjunction: Venus passes between Earth and the Sun
  • Superior Conjunction: Venus passes behind the Sun from Earth's perspective

These points are significant in astrology and can be used for timing and interpretation purposes.

Technical Details

The calculations use Swiss Ephemeris and follow astronomical algorithms to determine the exact times of Sun-Venus conjunctions. The system respects your current configuration settings for tropical/sidereal zodiac calculations.
