Progressive Calendar

The Progressive Calendar is a calendar that shows the transits and secondary directions for a given period.

Options

Default, the current date is used as the first date of the period but you can change that date.

The period is 33 days but you can choose a period of 100 days.

Initially, Enigma only shows transits but you can include secondary directions.

Enigma also offers an option to include parallels and contra-parallels.

The celestial points to include, and the orbs, are retrieved from the configuration: both the radix and the progressive configuration. The aspects are defined by the radix configuration.

After changing one of the options, all data is automatically recalculated.

Calendar as a list

The first tab, List, shows the calendar as a list. It contains the date and time (in UT) when an aspect is exact, the Method (mostly a Transit but it can be a Secondary Direction), the glyphs for the progressive point, the aspect and the radix point, and the longitude of the progressive point.

Calendar as a graph

The second tab, Aspects graph, shows a graph with the aspects that are within orb. Transits are presented with a blue line and secondary directions by a green line.
